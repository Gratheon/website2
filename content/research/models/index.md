Gratheon trains and maintains machine-learning models for digital beekeeping and hive monitoring.

## Available models

### [Bee detection](/research/models/bee-detection/)

Object-detection model and weights for detecting bees in hive entrance imagery.

### [Queen bee detection](/research/models/queen-bee-detection/)

In-house object detector for finding queen bees among worker bees, drones, pollen bees, and frame/background content.

### [BeePose](/research/models/beepose/)

Bee pose-estimation model with 13 key points for behaviour and movement analysis.

### [Varroa-on-bee detection](/research/models/varroa-on-bee-detection/)

In-house model and microservice for detecting varroa mites directly on bees in hive images.
