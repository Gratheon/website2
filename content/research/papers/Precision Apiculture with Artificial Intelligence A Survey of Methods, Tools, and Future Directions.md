## Relevancy to Gratheon

This review is useful for mapping Gratheon's research backlog across Varroa diagnostics, entrance behavior, audio monitoring and multi-sensor systems. Co-author Aleksejs Zacepins belongs to a precision-beekeeping team already represented in the collection. The bibliography and tool inventory provide focused citation trails, while the discussion of generalization and deployment constraints can inform requirements for field trials.

## Evidence and limitations

Treat this as a survey and discovery aid, not a benchmark proving the accuracy of the systems it mentions. Its Artificial Bee Colony optimization discussion is not itself evidence of an implemented hive-monitoring method. Individual tool, dataset and performance claims should be checked against their original papers before product adoption.

## Reproducibility and provenance

The review identifies projects and research tools, but does not introduce a new codebase, benchmark dataset or validated detector. Publisher and Crossref metadata agree on online publication on 29 August 2026, in volume 55, issue 350, pages 79-97.
