---
title: "☀️ Solar radiance as weather datasource"
---

Hypothesis is that the sun can influence bee behaviour as well as plants secreting the nectar.

- So if we can get solar radiance around the apiary into telemetry analytics in the web app, we could allow beekeepers to correlate something useful. Solar radiance likely is a highly aggregated datasource by lat/lon coordinates and very dependent on time-of-the-year
