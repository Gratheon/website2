---
title: "🦀 Varroa infestation management"
---

This is a parent feature of

- [varroa mite detection](varroa-mite-detection.md)
- [Varroa Treatment diary](../free-tier/varroa-treatment-diary.md)

But also includes

- showing varroa mites in [Frame side management](../free-tier/frame-side-management.md) views
- varroa infestation % metric
    - calculated as amount of varroa mites / amount of bees. If its >100%, keep 100%.
- showing infestation metric in hive list
    - ability to sort hives by infestation percentage
- showing infestation metric in [Apiary position sharing](hobbyist-tier-ideas/apiary-position-sharing.md)
    - use median value across all hives
