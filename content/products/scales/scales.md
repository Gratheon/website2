<p class="knowledge-standfirst">🌡️ Beehive scales are a low stand that goes under a hive. A single load cell and an ESP32 pod flush in its side measure hive weight, brood-nest temperature, ambient climate and battery health every 10 minutes, and send the readings to the <a href="../web_app/index.md">web app</a>. The solar panel is the landing board (or a wing on the sunny side), no cables hang outside, and the same scale fits into the <a href="../robotic_beehive/robotic_beehive.md">Robotic Beehive</a> and works with the future Entrance Observer.</p>

The hardware is described in the [beehive sensors docs](../../docs/beehive-sensors/beehive-sensors.html): [product description](../../docs/beehive-sensors/product-description.md), [bill of materials](../../docs/beehive-sensors/bill-of-materials.md) and [production wiring](/docs/beehive-sensors/#wiring). The app stores readings in [hive telemetry storage](../web_app/pro-tier/hive-telemetry-storage.md) and compares them through [timeseries data analytics](../web_app/pro-tier/timeseries-data-analytics.md).

### What it measures
- **Hive weight**, 200 kg capacity: nectar flow, swarming (a sudden 1–3 kg drop), winter stores, robbing and theft
- **Brood-nest temperature** from a probe pushed in through the entrance: brood rearing, a queenless colony, the winter cluster
- **Ambient temperature and humidity** behind louvres in the base wall, for weather context
- **Battery, signal and firmware health**, so the app warns before data stops

### Design
- **Solar landing board:** the panel bees land on, in front of the entrance. It sits on the base, so nothing needs moving during an inspection
- **No loose cables:** load cell, sensors and connector are wired inside the base
- **Flush pod:** the ESP32 pod sits in the side of the base under the deck overhang, with nothing for snow or a boot to catch. Press its button to see the latest readings on a small display
- **Easy power:** swap the battery cartridge or charge over USB-C at sites without sun
- **Modular front:** landing boards, the Entrance Observer and the Robotic Beehive all use the same rail and connector
- **Solar where the sun is:** the panel is the landing board when the entrance faces south, or hooks onto the south-facing side as a wing
- **Wood you can build:** film-faced plywood deck and thermo-pine case from any joinery shop, 3D-printed small parts
- AP62AFB single-point load cell with overload stops under every corner, and a deck skirt that sheds rain and snow
- Ships calibrated in one flat box, installs in about 15 minutes
