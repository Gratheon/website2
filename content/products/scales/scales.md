<p class="knowledge-standfirst">🌡️ Beehive scales are a low stand that goes under a hive. A single load cell and a snap-in ESP32 pod measure hive weight, brood-nest temperature, ambient climate and battery health every 10 minutes, and send the readings to the <a href="../web_app/index.md">web app</a>. They run for months on 18650 cells or all year with a small solar panel, and the same scale fits into the <a href="../robotic_beehive/robotic_beehive.md">Robotic Beehive</a>.</p>

The hardware is described in the [beehive sensors docs](../../docs/beehive-sensors/beehive-sensors.html): [product description](../../docs/beehive-sensors/product-description.md), [bill of materials](../../docs/beehive-sensors/bill-of-materials.md) and [production wiring](/docs/beehive-sensors/#wiring). The app stores readings in [hive telemetry storage](../web_app/pro-tier/hive-telemetry-storage.md) and compares them through [timeseries data analytics](../web_app/pro-tier/timeseries-data-analytics.md).

### What it measures
- **Hive weight**, 200 kg capacity: nectar flow, swarming (a sudden 1–3 kg drop), winter stores, robbing and theft
- **Brood-nest temperature** from a probe on the top bars: brood rearing, a queenless colony, the winter cluster
- **Ambient temperature and humidity** in a radiation shield, for weather context
- **Battery, signal and firmware health**, so the app warns before data stops

### Design
- Aluminium deck with a skirt over a base pan: rain runs off, and nothing but the load cell carries the hive
- AP62AFB single-point load cell with overload stops under every corner
- IP67 pod with an ESP32-S3, 2–4 × 18650 cells and sealed M8 connectors, docked at the back away from the flight path
- Setup from a phone over BLE; uploads over Wi-Fi, with LoRa as an option
- 400 × 300 mm M10 mounting pattern shared with the Robotic Beehive plinth
