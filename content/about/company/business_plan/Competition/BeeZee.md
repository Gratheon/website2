## Overview

BeeZee is a Smart Beehive Monitoring System project from the University of Peradeniya Department of Computer Engineering. It is a student/academic prototype rather than a commercial product, but it is technically relevant because it combines cameras, sensors and AI.

## Product Features

- Real-time hive monitoring with live camera footage
- Historical data analysis
- Sensor suite for temperature, humidity, weight/bee activity and CO2/audio components in the budget
- Web/mobile-style interface and dashboard mockups
- AI-based anomaly detection for abscondment and feeding irregularities
- Raspberry Pi 4 and Raspberry Pi high-quality camera architecture
- Solar/battery-powered enclosure concept
- Public user manual, design manual and repository references

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Uses cameras and AI, closer to Gratheon's long-term direction than simple scale products
- Transparent architecture, budget and testing information
- Demonstrates feasibility of low-cost Raspberry Pi monitoring
- Academic openness can inspire prototypes and architecture decisions

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Not a commercial company or supported product
- Prototype hardware is bulky and may not be field-hardened
- No go-to-market, support, manufacturing or compliance evidence
- AI appears project-level rather than production-grade

### Strategic Implications

BeeZee is not a market threat, but it validates that low-cost vision + sensor monitoring is a natural direction for smart beehives. Gratheon can learn from its architecture while competing on production reliability, UX, deployability and domain-specific AI models.
