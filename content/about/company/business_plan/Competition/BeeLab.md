## Overview

BeeLab Technology Sweden AB is a Swedish digital beekeeping operating system. The current product is primarily a browser-based web application for planning, inspections, traceability, sales and association/community workflows, with optional IoT sensor integration.

## Product Features

- Browser-based app for mobile, tablet and desktop; no app installation required
- Apiary planning with annual wheel/calendar and seasonal advice
- Digital hive model with boxes, frames, history and detailed frame inspections
- Voice notes and QR-code workflows at the hive
- Harvest, bottling, batch, inventory, label and honey traceability support
- Sales support with price lists, receipts and Swish QR codes
- Association/district features: shared apiaries, bulletin board and member communication
- Optional integrated IoT sensor system for temperature, weight, humidity and more

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Very complete business-process coverage beyond simple hive notes
- Strong localization for Sweden, including traceability/legal compliance workflows
- Works in a browser across devices, lowering installation friction
- Community/association features can create network effects
- Optional IoT path lets users grow from software into telemetry

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Country-specific positioning; not obviously international yet
- No visible computer vision or entrance-camera analytics
- IoT is optional rather than core to the product story
- Compliance and sales features may be overkill for hobbyists outside Sweden

### Strategic Implications

BeeLab is a strong reminder that professional beekeepers need more than sensor graphs: traceability, inventory, sales, association workflows and legal reporting matter. Gratheon can differentiate with AI/vision, but should not ignore operational workflows around honey batches, labels, treatments and compliance.
