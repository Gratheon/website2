## Overview

Hivemind is a New Zealand remote beehive-monitoring company. It started with crowdfunding/affordable wireless beehive scales and now offers a broader product line for honey production, pollination and queen breeding operations.

## Product Features

- Satellite hub for remote communications
- Hive weight scales reporting every 6 hours
- Hive strength monitor reporting in-hive humidity, temperature and bee population entering/leaving the hive hourly
- Raingauge
- Online account and API documentation
- Pricing estimator with report-frequency and billing-period options
- Product brochures and support material for beekeepers and growers

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Strong remote-apiary communications story with satellite hub
- Product line goes beyond weight into bee counts and hive strength
- Commercial/pollination positioning can justify higher hardware spend
- API documentation indicates integration readiness
- New Zealand/Australia market presence and distributor workflow

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Expensive hardware compared with many EU hobbyist/semi-pro solutions
- No visible frame-level visual inspection
- Bee count/hive strength appears sensor-based rather than video-based behavior recognition
- Geographic support strongest outside Gratheon's initial Baltic/EU focus

### Strategic Implications

Hivemind is a serious telemetry competitor for commercial remote apiaries. Gratheon can avoid direct price competition at the high end initially and instead target EU semi-professionals with lower-cost hardware, stronger AI interpretation and visual diagnostics.
