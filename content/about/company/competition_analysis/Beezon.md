## Overview

BeeZon Smart Software IoT Solutions is a Greek company founded in 2015. Its Beezon Scale is a remote apiary-monitoring system based on precision beekeeping principles, offered in SMS and GPRS/internet versions.

## Product Features

- SMS version sends periodic hive measurements to selected recipients
- Tracks daily weight change, internal hive temperature, ambient temperature and humidity
- 100 kg or 150 kg certified load sensor with ±20 g resolution
- Humidity sensor and internal/external temperature sensors
- GPS location for theft events
- Alarm connection option
- Optional connection of up to two additional scales
- IP65 electronics enclosure, metal construction and weather-resistant materials
- Rechargeable battery and optional photovoltaic connection for autonomy
- Claimed two-year warranty and technical support

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Local Greek product built around nomadic beekeeping needs
- SMS workflow works in areas where mobile data/cloud access is unreliable
- Theft/GPS and long battery autonomy are practical apiary features
- Strong ruggedness positioning for outdoor conditions

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Legacy SMS-centric UX can feel dated compared with modern dashboards
- No camera, computer vision or frame-level inspection
- Limited evidence of current international expansion
- Manual interpretation of weight/temperature data remains necessary

### Strategic Implications

BeeZon shows that ruggedness, theft alerts and low-connectivity operation matter in Mediterranean beekeeping. Gratheon should consider SMS/offline fallback alerts and anti-theft workflows, while differentiating through richer AI and visual evidence.
