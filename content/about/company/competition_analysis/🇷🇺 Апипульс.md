## Overview

Апипульс is a Russian comprehensive hive monitoring and alerting system. The site states it currently delivers only within Russia. The product combines wireless in-hive sensors, apiary scales, a control hub, cloud storage and mobile/web applications.

## Product Features

- Internal multi-sensors for temperature, humidity, sound spectrum and hive movement/orientation
- Sound analysis across selected frequency spectra for swarming, queen issues, stress, pests and general colony condition
- Apiary scales up to 200 kg with 100 g accuracy, plus external temperature/humidity
- Control hub with Wi-Fi 2.4/5 GHz and LTE 4G support
- BLE collection from sensors; hub range stated up to 25 m, expandable with repeaters
- Hub support for up to 200 sensors
- Cloud server, web cabinet and iOS/Android app
- Push notifications, archive, graphs, audio playback and map of apiaries
- Predictive modeling claims for swarming and hive health
- Optional camera connection to the hub mentioned as possible

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Broad sensor set: weight + microclimate + audio + movement/GPS/orientation
- Strong alerting and predictive-health positioning
- Hub architecture can support many sensors per apiary
- Sound recordings and spectral views provide richer diagnostics than simple scales
- Targets hobbyist, professional and large operators

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Russia-only delivery and geopolitical/commercial constraints reduce EU threat
- No visible entrance computer vision or frame-level image inspection
- Audio/telemetry inferences may be harder for beekeepers to verify than images
- Current pricing and availability require catalog/preorder verification

### Strategic Implications

Апипульс is a strong example of multi-modal telemetry and sound analytics. Gratheon should track audio as a complementary modality, but can differentiate in Europe with visual diagnostics, open-source trust and local EU compliance/support.
