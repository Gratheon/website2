## Overview

Beeing (ACME21 S.R.L.) is an Italian urban-beekeeping and sustainability company. Its main product is B-BOX, a safe/interactive urban beehive, complemented by adoption programs, educational projects, CSR services and remote bee-monitoring ambitions.

## Product / Service Features

- B-BOX urban beehive for balconies, gardens and educational use
- B-BOX Plus with swarm activation and beekeeper assistance
- Hive adoption and CSR programs for companies
- Urban apiaries, air biomonitoring and training experiences
- Beekeeping courses and community program
- Public mission includes designing sensors for remote bee monitoring
- Strong media/consumer design positioning

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Distinct consumer/urban beekeeping brand
- CSR and education channels can reach customers outside traditional beekeeping
- Physical hive product creates a tangible entry point
- International community claims across many countries

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Not primarily a precision-monitoring or AI analytics platform
- Urban/hobbyist B-BOX use case differs from productive semi-pro apiaries
- No visible advanced computer vision or frame inspection
- Sensor offering is less prominent than hive/adoption services

### Strategic Implications

Beeing is not a direct technical threat, but it shows demand for approachable, safe, educational beekeeping products. Gratheon can borrow the consumer-friendly onboarding/education approach while focusing on professional monitoring value.
