## Overview

Beetight was one of the early beekeeping record-keeping systems. After roughly 15 years, it closed: new signups stopped on 20 June 2024, the service became read-only on 1 October 2024, and the site states it closed on 2 February 2025 with user data deleted.

## Product Features (Historical)

- Web app plus mobile apps for beekeeping records
- Hive inspections and customizable fields
- Photos and mobile field entry
- Pro subscriptions
- Large historical user base according to the closure note

## Competitive Analysis

### Strengths

- Long-lived product with strong brand recognition among older digital beekeeping users
- Proved demand for digital hive records well before the current AI/IoT wave
- Mobile + web architecture was the right direction for field work

### Weaknesses vs Gratheon

- Closed and no longer a direct competitor
- Maintenance burden became unsustainable for a small team
- Manual record keeping only; no IoT/camera automation
- Legacy mobile APIs and aging architecture created product risk

### Strategic Implications

Beetight is a cautionary example. Beekeeping apps can gain loyal users, but long-term maintenance, mobile app updates and data portability are critical. Gratheon should design for sustainable infrastructure, exports and upgrade paths from the start.
