A living calendar of popular international and regional beekeeping events. Use it to pick a country or region, then confirm the current edition on the organizer's site before booking travel.

Dates move every year. The **usual season** is more stable than a specific city. Last reviewed: 8 September 2026.

EurBee and eurobee are different events: EurBee is the scientific European Conference of Apidology; eurobee is the professional trade fair in Friedrichshafen.

## Typical season

| When | What tends to happen |
| --- | --- |
| January | North American winter circuit: ABF conference and NAHBE expo. Nordic-Baltic NBBC often sits in late January or early February. |
| March | Apimell in Piacenza, Italy. SICAMM (dark European honey bee) is often in late March when it is an in-person year. |
| June | Australian Bee Congress in winter for the southern hemisphere. |
| July | Eastern Apicultural Society annual conference in the eastern US and Canada. |
| August to early September | Latin American FILAPI congress. Africa Bee Expo has recently been in early September. |
| September | EurBee (biennial) and COLOSS (often the days before EurBee). Apimondia prefers mid-September to mid-October. |
| October | BeeCome / European Beekeeping Congress, German-language beekeeper congress, UK National Honey Show (late October). |
| November | eurobee trade fair in Friedrichshafen. Apimondia 2027 is an exception and is in mid-November in Dubai. |

## Global

### Apimondia - World Beekeeping Congress

Largest global gathering of beekeepers, scientists, traders, and legislators, with a large ApiEXPO trade show. Run by the International Federation of Beekeepers' Associations.

- **Usual season:** every 2 years, preferably mid-September to mid-October. Host continent rotates.
- **Next edition:** 50th congress, 15-19 November 2027, Dubai, UAE (Dubai World Trade Centre).
- **Previous:** Copenhagen, 23-27 September 2025; Santiago, Chile, 4-8 September 2023.
- **Sites:** [apimondia.org](https://apimondia.org/events/congresses/), [Apimondia 2027](https://apimondia.org/apimondia-2027/), [apimondia2027.org](https://www.apimondia2027.org)

### COLOSS conference

Annual scientific meeting of the COLOSS (Prevention of honey bee COlony LOSSes) network. Often scheduled next to EurBee so researchers can attend both.

- **Usual season:** early September, annually.
- **Latest edition:** 22nd conference, 6-7 September 2026, Bologna, Italy (same venue as EurBee 11).
- **Site:** [coloss.org](https://coloss.org/)

## Europe

### EurBee - European Conference of Apidology

Biennial scientific conference for honey bee and wild bee research. Started in Udine in 2004. English is the working language.

- **Usual season:** September, every 2 years, rotating European city.
- **This edition:** EurBee 11, 7-10 September 2026, Bologna Congress Center, Italy.
- **Site:** [eurbee2026.com](https://www.eurbee2026.com/)

### eurobee - professional trade fair

Annual international beekeeping fair and Southern German Professional and Commercial Beekeeping Days. Organized by the German Professional and Commercial Beekeepers' Association (DBIB). About 7,200 visitors and 165+ exhibitors in 2025.

- **Usual season:** early November, every year, Friedrichshafen, Germany.
- **Next edition:** 6-8 November 2026, Messe Friedrichshafen.
- **Sites:** [berufsimker.de/eurobee](https://berufsimker.de/eurobee/), [Messe Friedrichshafen](https://www.messe-friedrichshafen.de/event/eurobee-26)

### BeeCome / European Beekeeping Congress

Practitioner-oriented European congress with an exhibition, hosted by national associations. The 2026 edition is in France.

- **Usual season:** early October. Cadence is not strictly annual; host country changes.
- **Next edition:** 1-4 October 2026, Agen, France (UNAF and L'Abeille Gasconne).
- **Site:** [beecomeagen2026.com](https://beecomeagen2026.com/en/)

### Apimell

Largest specialized beekeeping trade fair in Italy. Strong for equipment, hive products, and meeting European suppliers.

- **Usual season:** first weekend of March, every year, Piacenza.
- **Next edition:** 5-7 March 2027, Piacenza Expo.
- **Site:** [apimell.it](https://www.apimell.it/en/)

### National Honey Show (UK)

UK's premier honey show plus lecture convention, workshops, and trade hall. Late October at Sandown Park near London.

- **Usual season:** late October, every year.
- **Next edition:** 22-24 October 2026, Sandown Park, Esher, Surrey.
- **Site:** [honeyshow.co.uk](https://www.honeyshow.co.uk/)

### German-language beekeeper congress

Rotating congress for German-speaking associations (Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and neighboring regions). Separate from the smaller annual Deutscher Imkertag.

- **Usual season:** October.
- **Next edition:** 93rd Deutschsprachiger Imkerkongress, 8-10 October 2026, Dresden.
- **Site:** [deutscherimkerbund.de](https://deutscherimkerbund.de/deutschsprachiger-imkerkongress/)

### Nordic-Baltic Beekeeping Council (NBBC)

Annual meeting of Nordic and Baltic beekeeper associations, with the NBARS research symposium. Relevant for Gratheon as an Estonia-based team.

- **Usual season:** late January or early February, rotating among member countries.
- **Latest edition:** 29-30 January 2026, Tallinn, hosted by Eesti Mesinike Liit. Next host rotates among member countries.
- **Site:** [mesinikeliit.ee](https://mesinikeliit.ee/nbbc-konverents-2026/) (host pages change by year)

### SICAMM

International association for conservation of the dark European honey bee (*Apis mellifera mellifera*). Mix of in-person congresses and online years.

- **Usual season:** late March when in person. Next in-person host announced for Ireland in 2027.
- **Site:** [sicamm.org](https://www.sicamm.org/events)

## North America

### American Beekeeping Federation (ABF)

Major US industry conference and tradeshow for commercial and sideline beekeepers.

- **Usual season:** January, every year, rotating US city.
- **Next edition:** 12-16 January 2027, Moody Gardens, Galveston, Texas.
- **Site:** [abfnet.org](https://abfnet.org/2027-abf-conference/)

### North American Honey Bee Expo (NAHBE)

Large winter expo in Louisville: vendors, education, honey show. Easy to combine with the January US circuit.

- **Usual season:** January, every year, Louisville, Kentucky.
- **Next edition:** 7-9 January 2027, Kentucky Expo Center.
- **Site:** [nahbexpo.com](https://www.nahbexpo.com/)

### Eastern Apicultural Society (EAS)

Largest non-commercial beekeeping education conference in the US. Lectures, workshops, Master Beekeeper exams, vendors. Rotates among 26 eastern US states and Canadian provinces.

- **Usual season:** late July, every year.
- **Latest edition:** 26-31 July 2026, Shepherdsville, Kentucky. Next summer host is announced on the EAS site.
- **Site:** [easternapiculture.org](https://easternapiculture.org/)

### Western Apicultural Society (WAS)

Education network for the western US and western Canada. Currently runs twice-monthly virtual mini-conferences rather than one large annual in-person congress.

- **Usual season:** 2nd and 4th Thursdays, year-round (virtual).
- **Site:** [westernapiculturalsociety.org](https://www.westernapiculturalsociety.org/)

## Latin America

### FILAPI - Latin American Beekeeping Congress

Main regional congress for Latin American national beekeeping organizations. Scientific program plus APIEXPO.

- **Usual season:** late August to early September. Cadence is multi-year; host country rotates.
- **Latest edition:** XVII congress, 31 August - 6 September 2026, Mar del Plata, Argentina (SADA + FILAPI). Next host is announced on FILAPI.
- **Site:** [filapi.org](https://filapi.org/)

## Africa and Middle East

### Africa Bee Expo

Continental conference and exhibition for African beekeeping, with international guests. Host country changes.

- **Usual season:** early September in recent editions.
- **Latest edition:** 2-7 September 2026 (exhibition 3-6 September), SAFEX, Algiers, Algeria.
- **Site:** [Apimondia announcement](https://apimondia.org/africa-bee-expo-2026/) (no single stable organizer domain yet)

### Apimondia 2027 in Dubai

See the global Apimondia entry. This is the next world congress and the main Middle East date on the calendar through 2027.

## Asia-Pacific

### Australian Bee Congress

National congress of the Australian Honey Bee Industry Council. Southern-hemisphere winter dates.

- **Usual season:** June, every few years, rotating Australian city.
- **Latest edition:** 5th congress, 17-20 June 2026, Adelaide Convention Centre.
- **Site:** [australianbeecongress.com.au](https://australianbeecongress.com.au/)

Apimondia also rotates to Asia (for example Daejeon, South Korea, 2015). There is no single annual pan-Asian congress with the same stability as EurBee or FILAPI; check [apimondia.org/events](https://apimondia.org/events/) for regional symposia.

## How to choose

1. **Meet commercial beekeepers and suppliers:** eurobee, Apimell, NAHBE, ABF, ApiEXPO at Apimondia.
2. **Meet scientists:** EurBee, COLOSS, Apimondia scientific sessions.
3. **Stay close to home (Baltic / Nordics):** NBBC, then BeeCome or eurobee.
4. **One trip, several events:** COLOSS is often the days before EurBee (Bologna 2026 is the current pair). In North America, NAHBE and ABF sit in the same January window.

Always re-check registration, visa, and exhibition booth deadlines on the official site. This page is a map, not a ticket.
