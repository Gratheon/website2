The Gratheon beehive scale is a low stand that goes under a hive. It has a single load cell, an ESP32 pod that sits flush in its side, and a solar panel that doubles as the landing board. Every 10 minutes it measures hive weight, brood-nest temperature, ambient temperature and humidity, and battery health. No cables run outside the case, and nothing has to be unplugged for an inspection. This section describes the Phase 3 production kit. The bench and field prototypes that led to it are summarised in Earlier prototypes.

    What it measures

    SignalSensorWhat the beekeeper learns
    Hive weightAP62AFB single-point load cell + HX711Nectar flow, the day a swarm leaves (a 1–3 kg drop in minutes), winter stores running low, robbing, theft or a hive knocked over.
    Brood-nest temperatureDS18B20 probe pushed in through the entranceBrood rearing (steady 34–35 °C), a queenless or failing colony (temperature drifts), where the winter cluster sits.
    Ambient temperature and humiditySHT40 in a louvred pocket in the base wallContext for weight and brood readings: foraging weather, cold snaps, damp periods.
    Device healthMAX17048 fuel gauge, Wi-Fi RSSI, reset reasonWhen to change batteries, and why data stopped arriving.

    Key figures

    Deck560 × 510 mm film-faced plywood with a thermo-pine skirt, fits a 506 × 450 mm (Estonian) hive with corner locators
    MaterialsPlywood and thermo-pine from a joinery shop, 3D-printed ASA parts, aluminium only for the load-cell brackets and rails
    Height112 mm scale stack + 25 mm levelling feet
    Capacity200 kg (350 kg cell option), overload stops under every deck corner
    ControllerESP32-S3-MINI-1 on a Gratheon carrier PCB, Wi-Fi + BLE, LoRa footprint
    Power2.5 W solar panel as the landing board or as a wing on the south-facing side, 2–4 × 18650 in a swappable cartridge, USB-C charging on the pod face
    Battery lifeabout 9 months (2 cells) or 13 months (4 cells) with no charging at all, at 10-minute readings and 30-minute uploads, including self-discharge and a cold-winter derate
    PodIP67 ASA, flush in the side of the base; 0.96″ OLED, sealed button, USB-C, battery cartridge
    Front interfaceRail + M12 8-pin connector shared by landing boards, the Entrance Observer and the Robotic Beehive harness
    ShippingOne flat box, about 600 × 550 × 160 mm and 14 kg, calibrated, with a captive transport lock
    Mounting4 × M10 on a 400 × 300 mm pattern, the same as the Robotic Beehive deck pads
    Target BOM≈ €150–250 at 100 units (see bill of materials)

    Details, reasoning and acceptance tests are in the product description. The dimensions of the AP62AFB cell and brackets come from the supplier listing and must be checked on the delivered part before the deck and base drawings are released.

    Front modules

    Everything electrical is fixed to the base, the part that is not weighed. Modules hook onto rails on the base with two hooks and a thumb screw (front for landing boards and the Observer, any side for the solar wing), and plug into one M12 connector underneath. The pod reads each module's ID chip, so there is nothing to configure.

    ModuleWhat it doesPower
    Solar landing board (default)The landing board is a 2.5 W ETFE panel with a textured surface, 6° slope, probe socket on its rear edge. Folds flat for shipping and folds down if hit.Charges the pod
    Solar wingThe same panel on a wing bracket, hooked on the left, back or right rail, whichever faces south, tilted 50°. Its lead runs under the base to the landing board.Charges the pod
    Plain landing boardWhite HDPE board used with a wing or lid panel, with no solar, and as the camera background for the Entrance Observer.—
    Lid panelSecond panel position on the hive lid, one lead clipped down the front corner into the landing board.Charges the pod
    Entrance Observer (concept)Camera arch over a plain landing board on the same rail. Brings mains or PoE power, shares readings with the pod over UART.Powers the pod
    Robotic Beehive harnessNo landing board; the cabinet has its own entrance. 5 V from the robot rail and roof panel, UART to the Jetson.Powers the pod

    Connector pinout, installation, battery swap and shipping are described in the product description.

    Robotic Beehive fit

    The scale is designed as a module of the Robotic Beehive. Both models use the same axes and hive dimensions, and the interface is fixed:

    InterfaceStand-alone scaleIn the Robotic Beehive
    FootprintDeck 560 × 510 mm, base 504 × 454 mmFits between the 22 mm corner posts (722 × 582 mm clear).
    Mounting4 levelling feet, M10, 400 × 300 mmFeet removed; the same four M10 holes bolt to the plinth deck cross members, where the rubber pads were.
    Height112 mm stackThe hive deck rises 112 mm. In hive-model.js the hive base becomes plinth + 112 mm + bottom board; every lift height derives from it.
    Front interfaceSolar landing board on the railNo landing board. The robot harness plugs into the same M12 connector: 5 V from the robot rail (roof panel + battery), UART and WAKE to the Jetson.
    Electronics podFlush in the side bayStays in the bay and becomes the always-on supervisor, with LoRa fitted and the watchdog relay output.
    DataWeight, temperatures, humidity, battery to telemetry-apiThe same, plus a cross-check: during a lift, the drop in scale weight must match what the fork load cells report.

    Production wiring

    Everything inside the pod is on one carrier PCB. The pod mates with a dock connector in its bay; an internal harness in the base runs to the load cell, the ambient sensor and the front-module connector, so no cable is exposed outside the case.
