The Gratheon beehive scale is a low stand that goes under a hive. It has a single load cell and a snap-in ESP32 pod, and every 10 minutes it measures hive weight, brood-nest temperature, ambient temperature and humidity, and battery health. This section describes the Phase 3 production kit. The bench and field prototypes that led to it are summarised in Earlier prototypes.

    What it measures

    SignalSensorWhat the beekeeper learns
    Hive weightAP62AFB single-point load cell + HX711Nectar flow, the day a swarm leaves (a 1–3 kg drop in minutes), winter stores running low, robbing, theft or a hive knocked over.
    Brood-nest temperatureDS18B20 probe on the top barsBrood rearing (steady 34–35 °C), a queenless or failing colony (temperature drifts), where the winter cluster sits.
    Ambient temperature and humiditySHT40 in a radiation shieldContext for weight and brood readings: foraging weather, cold snaps, damp periods.
    Device healthMAX17048 fuel gauge, Wi-Fi RSSI, reset reasonWhen to change batteries, and why data stopped arriving.

    Key figures

    Deck540 × 490 mm aluminium, fits a 506 × 450 mm (Estonian) hive with corner locators
    Height88 mm scale stack + 25 mm levelling feet
    Capacity200 kg (350 kg cell option), overload stops under every deck corner
    ControllerESP32-S3-MINI-1 on a Gratheon carrier PCB, Wi-Fi + BLE, LoRa footprint
    Power2–4 × 18650 in parallel, BQ24074 solar charger, optional 1 W 6 V panel
    Battery lifeabout 9 months (2 cells) or 13 months (4 cells) without solar, at 10-minute readings and 30-minute uploads, including self-discharge and a cold-winter derate
    EnclosureIP67 ASA pod on a dock at the back, three sealed M8 field connectors
    Mounting4 × M10 on a 400 × 300 mm pattern, the same as the Robotic Beehive deck pads
    Target BOM≈ €140–220 at 100 units (see bill of materials)

    Details, reasoning and acceptance tests are in the product description. The dimensions of the AP62AFB cell and brackets come from the supplier listing and must be checked on the delivered part before the deck and base drawings are released.

    Robotic Beehive fit

    The scale is designed as a module of the Robotic Beehive. Both models use the same axes and hive dimensions, and the interface is fixed:

    InterfaceStand-alone scaleIn the Robotic Beehive
    FootprintDeck 540 × 490 mm, base 524 × 474 mmFits between the 22 mm corner posts (722 × 582 mm clear).
    Mounting4 levelling feet, M10, 400 × 300 mmFeet removed; the same four M10 holes bolt to the plinth deck cross members, where the rubber pads were.
    Height88 mm stackThe hive deck rises 88 mm. In hive-model.js the hive base becomes plinth + 88 mm + bottom board; every lift height derives from it.
    Electronics podDocks on the back of the baseMounts on a plinth bracket and becomes the always-on supervisor. It is powered from the robot's fused 5 V rail through the solar M8 port, with LoRa fitted, UART to the Jetson and the watchdog relay output.
    DataWeight, temperatures, humidity, battery to telemetry-apiThe same, plus a cross-check: during a lift, the drop in scale weight must match what the fork load cells report.

    Production wiring

    Everything inside the pod is on one carrier PCB. Field parts plug in through M8 connectors, so a damaged probe, load cell or panel is replaced without opening the pod. Click a wire or part for details; switch to 3D in the canvas corner.
