Phase 3 is the supportable product version. It replaces fragile prototype wiring with a repeatable enclosure, service connectors, a PCB or carrier, calibrated mechanics, diagnostics, and a path to gateway connectivity.

## Pages in this phase

- [Product description](product-description.md) - production architecture, connector strategy, mechanical requirements, service diagnostics, and gateway variants.
- [Bill of materials](bill-of-materials.md) - production-grade parts, sourcing options, and connectorized subsystem plan.
- [🧪 Research references](../research-references.md) - research papers and design implications behind the production roadmap.

## Phase outcome

Gratheon can assemble, calibrate, ship, support, and service multiple identical units with predictable telemetry, safe power handling, and replaceable field components.
