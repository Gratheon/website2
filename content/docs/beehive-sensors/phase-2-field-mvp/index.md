Phase 2 is the first outdoor pilot device. It turns the lab circuit into a weatherproof DIY hive scale that a beekeeper can install with common tools.

## Pages in this phase

- [Product description](product-description.md) - outdoor scope, electrical interfaces, mechanical stack, power budget, and installation flow.
- [Bill of materials](bill-of-materials.md) - parts to buy for a field pilot unit.

## Phase outcome

A pilot beekeeper can place a hive on the scale, power the device from battery or solar-assisted battery, pair it to a hive in Gratheon, and receive weight, temperature, humidity, battery, and connectivity readings.
