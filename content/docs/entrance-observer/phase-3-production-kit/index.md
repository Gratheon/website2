Phase 3 converts the field MVP into a repeatable Entrance Observer kit that Gratheon can sell, support, update, and manufacture. The production design should be selected from measured field data, not from developer-board convenience.

## Pages in this phase

- [Product description](product-description.md) - production architecture, SKU strategy, component decisions, and quality gates.
- [Bill of materials](bill-of-materials.md) - repeatable production BOM with alternatives and sourcing rules.

## Phase outcome

Gratheon has a supportable entrance-observer SKU with documented compute, camera, enclosure, power, network, connector, telemetry, video, QA, and service requirements. The product can be installed repeatedly by non-developers and can survive outdoor apiary operation.
