Interactive wiring diagram

The schematic is the electrical interconnect. Switch to 3D to orbit the bench. Solid lines are the default lab path. Dashed lines are mechanical joints or optional field/bench extras. Optics (lens, acrylic window) and WiFi antennas stay on the BOM, not on this diagram.

Power rule: Official Orin Nano power is the DC barrel at 19 V, not USB-C and not 5 V USB. USB-C on this carrier is data/recovery only. The camera is bus-powered from USB 3. The HDMI panel is optional, bench-only, and needs a DisplayPort-to-HDMI adapter.

Pre-power checklist

First power-on sequence

  1. Connect Ethernet, NVMe and the camera USB cable before applying PD power.
  2. Plug the 19 V barrel into Jetson POWER, then into the wall.
  3. Confirm the carrier LEDs come up and the camera appears with v4l2-ctl --list-devices.
  4. Start entrance-observer on a known clip or live UVC source.
  5. If the camera drops, swap the USB cable before changing model or tracker settings.
