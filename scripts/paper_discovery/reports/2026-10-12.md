6. **Publisher/repository sources:** arXiv version records, DergiPark publisher record and PDF, Research Square PDF, Zenodo file lists and GitHub repository metadata. One targeted Brave title query supplemented discovery. No paywall bypass or piracy source was used.

Search responses and full extraction artifacts are cached locally under `.cache/research-2026-10-12/` and are not committed. Durable source identifiers and PDF provenance are recorded here and in `2026-10-12-downloads.json`.

### Evidence and limitations carried into the notes

- **Queen state:** 5,129 recordings, 3,285 hives, 47 apiaries. The 2D modulation model falls from AUROC/AUPRC 0.81/0.37 on unseen hives to 0.63/0.26 on unseen apiaries. These are not accuracy percentages or production guarantees. The authors are affiliated with INRS-EMT, Université du Québec and Nectar Technologies. Public code/data release was not verified.
- **BeeWhere:** 483 annotated frames and 8,443 bee instances; University of Copenhagen, Lund University and University of Wisconsin-Madison. Verified [annotation repository](https://github.com/robertahunt/bumblebox_annotator) and [model/annotation record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21870158). The Zenodo record specifies CC BY 4.0, while GitHub metadata did not establish a software license. The note explicitly distinguishes Bombus impatiens microcolonies from field honeybee monitoring and flags the paper's mixed split descriptions (video separation versus same-video frame spacing) for reproducibility review.
- **RSS tracking:** University of Sheffield; 38 mg archival receiver including power source, rotating transmitters, probabilistic offline path reconstruction. Reported approximately 15 m error below 180 microwatts and 10 m below 600 microwatts are evaluation results, not demonstrated live-cloud tracking accuracy on free-flying honeybees. Species demonstration: Bombus terrestris. arXiv's distribution license does not authorize Gratheon to mirror the PDF.
- **Pollen:** Marmara University; volume 28(84), pages 443-448, checked against PDF and publisher metadata. The 714-image collection is split into 571 training and 143 test images; 99.4% F1 is an in-dataset result. The note does not conflate random image splitting with cross-apiary validation or segmentation scores with whole-bee classification accuracy. Newly created masks/checkpoint availability was not verified.

## Candidates not added

- **A Precision Beekeeping Ontology**, https://doi.org/10.1177/15705838261461020, Applied Ontology, 2026-07-28. Directly relevant semantic interoperability work; Crossref verified title/authors/date. OpenAlex points to an accepted manuscript at Edinburgh Napier's repository, but that endpoint returned HTTP 403. No valid PDF obtained during this run. Deferred, not added.
- **Probabilistic Fundamental Frequency Estimation Enables Robust Bioacoustic Tracking and Caste Classification in Honey Bees**, https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11015495/v1, 2026-10-01. Legal CC BY 4.0 PDF downloaded and parsed; Research Square HTML returned HTTP 403. The PDF claims open code but the availability statement provides it only upon reasonable request. Its cited IVBEE-1.0 dataset identifier, `10.5281/zenodo.15783267`, returned HTTP 404 through the Zenodo records API. Evaluation is based on isolated specimens in an anechoic chamber, not actual hive noise. Deferred for verification of reproducibility and evidence behind the edge/noisy-hive claims rather than treating them as established.
- **In-hive Varroa destructor imaging: dataset, mite-level re-inspection layer, cross-validation splits, evaluation code and model weights**, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23276947, 2026-10-10, latest version under concept DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23099411`. High-value dataset with a cautionary annotation audit, but the authoritative file list contains README, image/weight/code ZIPs and no paper PDF. Not added as a paper. In-hive positives are especially weak: only 3 of 23 were confirmed in re-inspection; bee-class boxes must not be mistaken for tight mite annotations.
- **Determining blooming-phase dynamics through intra-day hive-weight analysis**, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atech.2026.102559. Relevant single-apiary study. OpenAlex discovery date is 2026-09-09, while Crossref lists a December 2026 issue and a CC BY-NC-ND license start of 2026-09-08. No publisher full text/PDF was verified in this bounded run; deferred rather than assigning the future issue date as an already-published online date or copying an NC-licensed file.
- **When the hive speaks: detecting Varroa destructor infestation in honey bees through acoustic biomarkers and machine learning**, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10493-026-01179-y. Rediscovered; OpenAlex still exposes no OA location. Previously deferred for unavailable legal PDF. Not added.
- Already-collected works such as the annotation-audit concept/version records, Braid, clover-foraging monitoring and modulation-tensorgram colony-strength paper were excluded as duplicates.
- Generic artificial-bee-colony optimization, bee-inspired UAV communication without bee interaction, pure ecology/toxicology, and unverified low-quality outlet results were rejected. Dataset-only records are not promoted into paper notes without a scholarly document and legal PDF.
