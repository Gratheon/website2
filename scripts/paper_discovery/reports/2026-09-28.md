# Research curation: 2026-09-28

## Scope and decisions

- Autonomous, one-off collection maintenance. No recurring job was created; weekly cadence remains a recommendation.
- Discovery window: 2026-06-30 through 2026-09-28. Older works were considered only when encountered through a citation/source trail.
- Initial collection: 164 top-level paper notes (excluding `index.md`) and 154 files in `pdfs/`.
- All existing notes were read programmatically. Duplicate checks covered Unicode/punctuation-normalized titles, DOI, arXiv IDs, URLs, linked PDF names, and files in `pdfs/`. Near-title matching was also used during discovery. Rechecked current notes before copying accepted PDFs.
- Final additions: two papers, two unmodified CC BY 4.0 PDFs. Expected collection: 166 notes and 156 PDF files.
- Kept work limited to curation, generated indexes, and lessons learned. Did not change discovery or ingestion logic.

## Sources searched and limitations

1. OpenAlex: date-bounded queries for precision beekeeping, smart beehives, honeybee monitoring/tracking, Varroa detection, computer vision, bee datasets, bee traffic, queenlessness acoustics, and waggle dance decoding. Some queries returned HTTP 429; subsequent successful queries supplied additional candidates. Results are not an exhaustive census.
2. Semantic Scholar: attempted honeybee-monitoring search; HTTP 429. Unavailable, not evidence of zero new papers.
3. Crossref: date-bounded beehive-monitoring search and DOI lookups. Braid metadata was confirmed. The clover paper's Crossref 404 was resolved by checking its actual registration agency, DataCite, which confirmed its title, year, publisher, and DOI.
4. arXiv: submitted-date-bounded honeybee/beehive/Varroa search. Relevant previously collected work was excluded; bee-inspired UAV communication and purely mathematical mite control were not accepted as bee-monitoring systems.
5. Europe PMC: date-bounded bee-monitoring/detection search with core metadata and abstracts. This supplied the accepted papers and additional candidates. Publisher PDFs, publisher JATS XML, author affiliations, review history, and code repositories were checked directly.
6. Targeted Brave searches and a September beekeeping digest supplied supplementary leads, not authoritative publication metadata. Followed those leads to primary records. A source/citation trail led to SOPAR, published in June and newly indexed in PubMed in September.

The accepted works were absent by title, DOI, source URL, and local PDF name. Both contain explicit honey bee monitoring demonstrations, not merely generic computer vision methods.

## Accepted papers

Scores use topic / novelty / practical relevance / source quality / open availability, with maxima 4 / 2 / 2 / 1 / 1.

### Low-latency multicamera 3D tracking of insects with Braid

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.21.745392
- Authors: Michael J. M. Harrap; Andrew D. Straw. University of Freiburg and Bernstein Center Freiburg, Germany.
- bioRxiv version 1, posted 2026-08-26; not peer reviewed.
- Score: **10/10** (4/2/2/1/1). Reproducible camera synchronization, calibration, and live 3D bee-tracking protocol with public source code.
- PDF: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.21.745392v1.full.pdf
- Code: https://github.com/strawlab/strand-braid
- Redistribution evidence: CC BY 4.0 statement on the source page and PDF itself. Paper note attributes both authors and links the license/source.
- Local file: `2026-braid-multicamera-insect-tracking.pdf`, 28,787,223 bytes.
- SHA-256: `4baa0ba38b09479112dc60b5d1ee76eb3a1c1d510c078460e34723bd3d9d9e1c`.
- Caveat: submillimeter accuracy is an arena-specific report, not a promise for crowded or outdoor hive entrances. No claim of persistent bee identity.

### Digital Monitoring and Modeling of Honey Bee Foraging on Clover Flowers Under Heat Stress

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.17912/micropub.biology.002378
- Authors: Peter Oliver, Sushma Naithani, Joussy Hidrobo-Chavez, Yashoda Murali, Benjamin Nichols, Ramesh Sagili, Pankaj Jaiswal, Yue Zhang. Oregon State University, USA.
- microPublication Biology, published 2026-08-27, accepted 2026-08-24; publisher records anonymous peer review.
- Score: **9/10** (4/1/2/1/1). Direct pollination-monitoring use, public analysis scripts, and transparent within-dataset evaluation limitations.
- PDF: https://www.micropublication.org/static/pdf/micropub-biology-002378.pdf
- Code: https://github.com/naithanis/Naithani-lab-codes/tree/master/Clover-honeybees
- Redistribution evidence: CC BY 4.0 in publisher JATS XML and PDF copyright statement. Paper note credits the authors and original source.
- Local file: `2026-clover-honeybee-foraging-monitoring.pdf`, 7,762,239 bytes.
- SHA-256: `bac376f7e6b1695324f4ba8f183e55c46f1fb7a63eb12bbe907338ec2c46293b`.
- Caveats: models tested on subsets of the same image collections. The note uses methods-section precision 0.922 and mAP50 0.953 rather than confusing the latter with the introductory claim of 95% precision. Heat-stress causality is not established.
