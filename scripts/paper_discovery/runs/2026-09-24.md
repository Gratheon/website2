# Research collection maintenance: 2026-09-24

## Scope and decisions

Autonomous maintenance run requested by the user. Discovery window: 2026-06-26 through 2026-09-24, inclusive. Older citation discoveries were allowed, but no older papers were selected. No recurring job was created: weekly cadence was guidance, not a request to configure scheduling.

Read all 158 top-level paper notes (excluding index.md) and inventoried 151 PDF-directory files. Deduplicated against normalized titles, DOI strings, arXiv identifiers without version suffixes, scholarly source URLs, and PDF basenames. Shared license and author-identity URLs are not paper identifiers. Collection after this run: 162 notes and two additional PDF files.

## Discovery and verification

- OpenAlex: date-bounded broad searches and more targeted relevance-ranked queries for precision beekeeping, smart beehives, acoustic monitoring, computer vision, Varroa detection, bee traffic, automatic waggle-dance analysis, and datasets. Both discovery approaches were used because sorting broad searches only by date hides relevant papers behind biology results.
- Semantic Scholar: search attempted, HTTP 429; no conclusion about coverage drawn from the failed request.
- Crossref: checked publisher metadata and licenses for journal candidates, including multimodal weight forecasting and label-free swarming.
- arXiv: date-bounded search plus ID lookup for 2608.23213. The initial vocabulary search missed that entrance-tracking paper; OpenAlex found it. The acoustic colony-strength paper 2607.20386 was already in the collection and was not added again.
- Europe PMC: recent bee-monitoring discovery and full-text XML for the structured computer-vision review. The first response contained 50 of 416 broad-query results; this was a selective run, not an exhaustive systematic review.
- Zenodo: checked the version-specific Varroa audit record, author ORCID, license, PDF, and reproducibility archive listing. Concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22923458 and version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22923459 are one work, not two additions.
- Publisher pages and PDFs: checked article identity, authors, affiliations where supplied, methods, reported results, and limitations. No paywall or bot challenge was bypassed.

Raw responses and downloaded verification files are local, gitignored artifacts in `.cache/paper-maintenance/`.

## Candidates not added

- **A Precision Beekeeping Ontology**, DOI 10.1177/15705838261461020: promising schema/interoperability reference. OpenAlex lists an Edinburgh Napier repository manuscript, but both publisher and repository access returned HTTP 403. No PDF verified.
- **Computer Vision for Monitoring Wild Bees and Wasps: A Structured Literature Review**, DOI 10.1002/ece3.73794: relevant reusable monitoring methods and CC BY full-text XML verified through Europe PMC. Publisher/Europe PMC PDF requests were blocked, and the PMC PDF endpoint returned HTML instead of PDF. Not added under this run's strict PDF-verification requirement.
- **When the hive speaks: detecting Varroa destructor infestation in honey bees through acoustic biomarkers and machine learning**, DOI 10.1007/s10493-026-01179-y: relevant recent discovery, but no legal open PDF verified in this run.
- **Air-based monitoring of honey bee colonies: system description and cage-disjoint validation of disease-detection models**, dataset record 10.5281/zenodo.22306866: the inspected record contains CSVs, code, README, and license, but no paper PDF. Retained as a candidate, not ingested as a scholarly PDF entry.
- Molecular diagnostics, bee-inspired UAV/optimization algorithms without real bee monitoring, and generic biological/ecological results were excluded. Unverified or low-confidence venues were not promoted merely because an open URL existed.
